Le Programme d'Études
The 2-Month Intensive Curriculum

Strategically designed to build confidence first (b chwiya b chwiya), then push to advanced expression — just like how Darija flows naturally into new ideas.

Phase 1: Foundation
التأسيس
🎯 Core Vocabulary & Simple Structures

Getting kids comfortable speaking without fear. We start with simple phrases so they can quickly express basic needs and ideas — no pressure, just progress.

  • Simple sentence structures
  • Speaking without fear of mistakes
  • Daily expressions bridging Darija → English

  • Fun challenges & gamified activities 🎮

اعرف مستوى ولدك · Know the Level
Quick Level Test 📝

Before joining, every child takes a short assessment so we can place them in exactly the right level. Answer 4 quick questions — it only takes 1 minute!

Question 1 of 4
Can your child say basic greetings in English? (Hello, Goodbye, How are you?)
Question 2 of 4
Can your child make a simple sentence? (Example: "I like football" / "My name is...")
Question 3 of 4
Can your child talk about their day or describe something in English?
Question 4 of 4
Can your child explain WHY they like something? (Example: "I like football because it's fun and exciting")
